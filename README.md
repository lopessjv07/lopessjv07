<p align="left">🚀 Chamou

Chamou is an instant job platform designed to connect companies and freelancers quickly, easily, and directly.
The goal is for companies to post immediate jobs and for available professionals to accept them in just a few clicks, without bureaucracy.

The application is being developed for Web (desktop and mobile) and Mobile (Android/iOS), with a focus on:
- ⚡ Development speed
- 🔐 User security (document, phone, and 2FA verification)
- 🎯 Good user experience (optimized UX)

Chamou is still in its early stages, but the vision is to become a practical solution for quick jobs and instant opportunities..<br>You can check in my linktree above.</p>

<p align="left">🚀 Ordemo - SaaS

A **multi-tenant** SaaS platform built to manage beauty businesses (salons, barbershops, and independent professionals), focused on scheduling, daily operations, and centralized administration.

### 🎯 Who it's for
- Salon and barbershop owners  
- Independent professionals  

### ✨ Key features

**Business management**
- Smart scheduling calendar  
- Staff/professionals and services management  
- Customer management  
- Business hours customization and date blocking  
- Financial control (income/expenses)  

**Customer experience**
- Online booking (public page)  
- Customer portal with booking history  

**Admin (Supreme)**
- Central dashboard for all tenants/stores  
- Subscription and billing management  
- Audit logs, notifications, and security insights
